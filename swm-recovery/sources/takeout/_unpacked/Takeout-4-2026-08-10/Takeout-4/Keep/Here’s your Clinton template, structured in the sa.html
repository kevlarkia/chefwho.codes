REQUIREMENTS

• Vary pacing: quick sarcastic quips mixed with slower reflective passages.

• Blend sharp analysis with biting jokes.

• Mix grounded observations with surreal exaggeration.
• Let the humor feel like armor—funny, but with edges showing.
