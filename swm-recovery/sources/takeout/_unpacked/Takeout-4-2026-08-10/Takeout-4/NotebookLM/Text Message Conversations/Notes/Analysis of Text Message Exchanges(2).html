Glossary of Key Terms:

  • Swatted: A harassment tactic involving making a false report to emergency services (like the police) to provoke a large law enforcement response at someone's address.
  • Cigs: An informal abbreviation for cigarettes.
  • Wyd: An initialism commonly used in text messaging and online communication, meaning "What you doing?"
  • Dealer: In this context, likely refers to someone who sells illegal drugs.
  • Coke: A common slang term for the drug cocaine.
  • Sac: Likely a shortened or informal reference to Sacramento, a city in California.
  • Bach: Potentially a shortened or misspelled version of "batch," though the subsequent message "The gross T?" suggests it might be a specific type or brand of substance being discussed.
  • Grissini: Long, thin, crisp breadsticks, often served as an appetizer.
  • Harissa: A hot chili pepper paste commonly used in North African and Middle Eastern cuisine.
  • Pesto Verde: A sauce made with basil, pine nuts, garlic, Parmesan cheese, and olive oil.
  • Bottoms Up: A toast used when drinking, wishing good health or fortune. In the context of the menu item, it implies something enjoyable and perhaps indulgent.
  • RCS: Rich Communication Services, an upgrade to traditional SMS text messaging that allows for features like read receipts, typing indicators, and larger file transfers.
  • Arrs u think ing: A phonetic spelling of "Are you thinking," commonly seen in informal text messaging.
  • Kmow: A phonetic spelling of "know," commonly seen in informal text messaging.
  • Haveing: A misspelling of "having," commonly seen in informal text messaging.
