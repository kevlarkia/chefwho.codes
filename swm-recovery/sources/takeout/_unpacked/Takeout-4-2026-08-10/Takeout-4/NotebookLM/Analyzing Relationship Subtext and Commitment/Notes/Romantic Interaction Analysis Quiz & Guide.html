Glossary of Key Terms

  • Subtext: An underlying and often unspoken meaning in a conversation or text.
  • Tone: The attitude of a speaker or writer, often conveyed through word choice, phrasing, and pacing.
  • Timing: The strategic use of pauses or delays in communication.
  • Emotional Charge: The intensity of feeling associated with a statement or interaction.
  • Micro-behaviors: Subtle, often unconscious physical or verbal cues that reveal underlying emotions or intentions.
  • Vulnerability: The willingness to be emotionally open and exposed.
  • Directness: The quality of being straightforward and unambiguous in communication.
  • Commitment Clarity: The degree to which intentions about future involvement are made clear.
  • Emotional Availability: The capacity and willingness to engage emotionally with another person.
  • Romantic Depth: The level of intimacy, connection, and potential for a serious romantic relationship.
  • Situationship: A romantic or sexual relationship that is not considered formal or established.
  • Deflection: The act of turning aside or redirecting attention, often to avoid a difficult topic.
  • Mismatch: A situation where two things do not fit well together or are incompatible.
