1. Transit Analysis: A Holistic Approach

To achieve a truly holistic transit analysis, it's crucial to move beyond simply listing individual transits and their interpretations. We need to focus on how the transits interact with each other and create overall patterns or themes.

Looking for Patterns:

**Clusters of Transits:**  Are there several transits happening in the same sign or house at the same time? This indicates a concentrated focus of energy in that area of life.

**Recurring Aspects:**  Are there certain aspects (e.g., squares, trines) that are being repeated or activated by different transiting planets? This suggests a recurring theme or challenge.

**Transits to Sensitive Points:**  Pay special attention to transits affecting the Sun, Moon, Ascendant, Venus, and Mars in both natal and composite charts, as these are highly sensitive points.

Considering the Bigger Picture:

**Long-Term vs. Short-Term:**  How do the long-term transits (Saturn, Jupiter, outer planets) set the stage for the short-term transits (Mars, Venus, Mercury)?

**Nodal Transits:**  Transits to the North and South Nodes of the Moon can indicate karmic themes or life lessons being activated.

**Eclipses:**  Eclipses can bring significant turning points or changes in the areas of life they affect.

Example:

If, during our timeframe, transiting Saturn in Pisces is squaring Clinton's Venus, but transiting Jupiter is also trining Clinton's Sun, we have a combination of challenging and supportive influences. This might indicate that Clinton is facing challenges in his love life (Saturn square Venus), but he also has opportunities for personal growth and expansion (Jupiter trine Sun) that can help him navigate those challenges.
