Secondary Progressions:

Secondary progressions are a symbolic method of time-keeping in astrology. They involve progressing the planets and points in the natal chart at a rate of one day for each year of life.

Progressions can reveal long-term trends and shifts in an individual's life and personality.

**How to use progressions in relationship analysis:**

    Look for progressions to Clinton and Marco's natal planets and composite chart points.

    Pay attention to changes in sign or house placement of progressed planets.

    Analyze aspects formed by progressed planets to natal or composite chart points.

**Example:**

    If Clinton's progressed Venus is moving into Virgo, this might indicate a shift in his approach to love and relationships, becoming more practical and detail-oriented.

    If the composite chart's progressed Moon is squaring the composite Saturn, this might indicate a period of emotional challenges or a need to bring more structure to the relationship.

Solar Returns:

A solar return chart is a chart calculated for the moment when the transiting Sun returns to its natal position each year.

Solar return charts provide insights into the themes and focuses for the year ahead.

**How to use solar returns in relationship analysis:**

    Look at Clinton and Marco's individual solar return charts for the year.

    Pay attention to the house placements of planets in the solar return charts.

    Analyze aspects formed by planets in the solar return charts.

**Example:**

    If Clinton's solar return chart has a strong emphasis on the 7th house (partnerships), this might indicate a year focused on relationships.

    If Marco's solar return chart has challenging aspects to Venus, this might indicate a year with potential challenges in his love life.

Important Note:

Predictive techniques are tools for gaining insights into potential future developments, but they are not deterministic.
