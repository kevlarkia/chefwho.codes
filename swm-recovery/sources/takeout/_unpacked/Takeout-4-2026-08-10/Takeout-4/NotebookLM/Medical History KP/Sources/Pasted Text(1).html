The document is a Mental Impairment Questionnaire designed to assess a patient's mental health and work-related limitations. ​

Patient Information

The questionnaire includes sections for patient details such as name, date of birth, and date completed. ​

It requires information about the frequency and length of contact with the patient.

Mental Health Assessment

Questions cover DSM-5 diagnoses, treatment responses, prescribed medications, and side effects. ​

Clinical findings and severity of mental impairment symptoms are documented. ​

Cognitive and Behavioral Symptoms

The questionnaire lists various cognitive and behavioral symptoms, including cognitive decline, hyperactivity, delusions, and mood disturbances.

It assesses the patient's intellectual functioning and expected limitations in a work setting. ​

Limitations and Prognosis

The document evaluates the degree of limitations in understanding, remembering, applying information, and interacting with others. ​

It addresses the chronicity of the mental disorder and the patient's ability to manage their own benefits. ​

Substance Abuse and Additional Factors

Questions regarding current or past substance abuse and its impact on impairments are included. ​

The questionnaire concludes with a section for comments and the signature of the medical provider.
