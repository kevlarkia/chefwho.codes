IV. Glossary of Key Terms

  • ABI (Ankle-Brachial Index): A test that compares blood pressure in the ankle with blood pressure in the arm to check for peripheral artery disease (PAD).
  • Atherectomy: A procedure to remove plaque from an artery.
  • Angioplasty: A procedure to widen a narrowed or blocked blood vessel.
  • DCB (Drug-Coated Balloon): A balloon used in angioplasty that is coated with medication to prevent the artery from narrowing again.
  • Claudication: Pain caused by too little blood flow, usually during exercise. Often affects the legs.
  • Ischemia: A condition in which the blood flow (and thus oxygen) is restricted or reduced in a part of the body.
  • Digital Ischemia: Lack of blood flow to the fingers or toes.
  • Anion Gap: The difference between the measured cations (positively charged ions) and the measured anions (negatively charged ions) in serum, plasma, or urine. It is used to help identify the cause of metabolic acidosis.
  • BUN (Blood Urea Nitrogen): A test that measures the amount of urea nitrogen in your blood, which is an indication of kidney function.
  • COPD (Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease): A chronic inflammatory lung disease that obstructs airflow from the lungs.
  • HDL (High-Density Lipoprotein): Often referred to as "good" cholesterol.
  • LDL (Low-Density Lipoprotein): Often referred to as "bad" cholesterol.
  • Macrocytsosis: A condition in which red blood cells are larger than normal.
  • Spondylosis: Degeneration of the spine.
  • Fibrumusclar Dysplasia (FMD): A condition that causes narrowing or blockages in the arteries.
  • mEq/L: Milliequivalents per liter, a unit of measurement for electrolytes.
  • D-Dimer: a blood test that measures a substance that is released when a blood clot breaks up
  • FEU: Fibrinogen Equivalent Units
  • PHQ9: Patient Health Questionnaire-9, a tool used to assess the severity of depression.
  • ICD-10-CM: International Classification of Diseases, Tenth Revision, Clinical Modification
  • SSN: Social Security Number
  • NPI: National Provider Identifier
  • MWS: Medical Web Services (Laboratory)
  • CBC + DIFF (AT REG LAB): Complete Blood Count with Differential (At Regular Lab)
  • ER: Emergency Room
  • AST: Aspartate Aminotransferase
  • ALT: Alanine Aminotransferase
  • HGB: Hemoglobin
  • HCT: Hematocrit
  • WBC: White Blood Cells
  • PLT: Platelet Count
  • TIBC: Total Iron Binding Capacity
  • CRP: C-Reactive Protein
