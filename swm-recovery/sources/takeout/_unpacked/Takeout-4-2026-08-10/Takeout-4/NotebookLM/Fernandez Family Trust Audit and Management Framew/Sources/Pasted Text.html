ARTICLE I RESTATEMENT OF TRUST

  1. [Restatement]{.underline}

FRANK G. FERNANDEZ and MARION L. FERNANDEZ are the Settlors of THE

FERNANDEZ FAMILY TRUST created under that certain trust agreement dated

June 8, 1994, as second amended and restated on June 29, 2012. FRANK G.

FERNANDEZ and MARION L. FERNANDEZ are the Trustees duly appointed and

acting under and by terms of that trust agreement. In Article IX of that

second amended and restated trust agreement, the Settlors reserved the

right to amend the trust.

The Settlors now wish to exercise their right of amendment and, to that

end, do hereby amend and restate that trust agreement in the terms set

forth in this amended and restated declaration of trust. The Trustees

hereby consent to the terms of this amended and restated agreement. The

parties agree that, upon execution of this instrument, that trust

agreement shall be replaced in whole, and the terms of this amended and

restated trust agreement shall supersede the terms of that trust

agreement for all purposes.

The Settlors and the Trustees confirm that all assets currently titled

in the name of THE FERNANDEZ FAMILY TRUST shall continue to be held by

the Trustees as trust assets of the amended and restated trust.

**ARTICLE **II

**CREATION OF **TRUST

  1. [Creation of Trust]{.underline}

FRANK G. FERNANDEZ and MARION L. FERNANDEZ, called the "Settlors" or

the ''Trustees", depending on the context, and sometimes referred to

as "Husband" and "Wife" respectively, declare that they have set

aside and hold in trust the property described in Schedule A attached to

this instrument.

Husband and Wife have accumulated significant amounts of property during

their marriage.

It is Husband and Wife's desire, by this instrument, to create an inter

vivos revocable trust in accordance with the laws of the State of

California whereby they will hold in trust their community property and

their separate property and manage it for their benefit during their

respective lives and provide for their issue upon the death of the last

survivor of Husband and Wife.

  1. [Names of Trusts]{.underline}

The trusts created in this instrument may be referred to collectively as

THE FERNANDEZ FAMILY TRUST, and each separate trust created in this

instrument may be referred to by adding the name of the beneficiary.

**ARTICLE **III

TRUST ESTATE

  1. [Definition of Trust Estate]{.underline}

All property subject to this instrument from time to time is referred to

as the trust estate and shall be held, administered, and distributed

according to this instrument. After the death of the first spouse, the

term "trust estate" shall not include the disclaimer trust.

  1. [Community Property in Trust]{.underline}

All community property transferred to the trust shall retain its

character as such notwithstanding the transfer to this instrument. Any

power reserved to the Settlors to alter, amend, modify or revoke this

trust, in whole or in part, is held by the Settlors during their joint

lifetimes in their capacity as managers of the community property,

subject to all restrictions imposed by law on their management of the

community property.

  1. [Separate Property in Trust]{.underline}

All separate property of either Settlor transferred to the trust that

was, before its transfer, the separate property of that Settlor shall

retain its character as such notwithstanding its transfer to this trust.

3.4 . [Quasi-Community Property in Trust]{.underline}

All quasi-community property of either Settlor transferred to the trust

that was, before its transfer, quasi-community property of that Settlor

shall retain its character as such notwithstanding its transfer to this

trust. For convenience, any quasi-community property transferred to this

trust is referred to as separate property of the Settlor to whom it

belonged

before the transfer; however, the Settlors do not intend by this

reference to change the character of this property from quasi-community

property to separate property.

3.5 [Joint Tenancy Property in Trust]{.underline}

Notwithstanding the foregoing, any joint tenancy property that becomes

subject to this trust shall thereby become community property to the

extent that the settlors' interests in the property were equal.

ARTICLE IV

DISTRIBUTION DURING SETTLORS' JOINT LIVES

  1. [Payment oflncome From Comm.unity Property]{.underline}

During the Settlors' joint lifetimes, the Trustees shall pay to or

apply for the benefit of both spouses for the account of the community

the entire net income of the community estate in convenient installments

at least annually.

  1. [Payment of Principal From Community Property]{.underline}

If the Trustees consider the net income to be insufficient, the Trustees

shall pay to or apply for the benefit of both spouses for the account of

the comm.unity as much of the principal of the community estate as the

Trustees consider necessary for the Settlors' health, education,

support, and maintenance to maintain at a minimum their accustomed

manner of living, without taking into consideration income and resources

available to both spouses held free of this trust.

The spouse receiving payments shall have the same duty to use community

income and principal received under this instrument for the Settlors'

benefit as he or she has with respect to any other community property.

Any community income or principal paid to the Settlors, applied for

their benefit, or withdrawn by them shall continue to retain its

character as community property.

  1. [Payment of Income From $0J)arate Property]{.underline}

During the Settlors' joint lifetimes, the Trustees shall pay to or

apply for the benefit of the Settlor the net income of that Settlor's

separate estate in convenient installments at least annually.

  1. [Payment of Principal from Separate Property]{.underline}

If the Trustees consider the net income of the separate property to be

insufficient for the Settlor who owns such property, the Trustees shall

pay to or apply for the benefit of the Settlor as much of the principal

of that Settlor's separate estate as the Trustees consider necessary

for the Settlor's health, education, support, maintenance, comfort,

welfare, or happiness to

maintain at a minimum the Settlor's accustomed manner of living,

without taking into consideration funds and assets available to the

Settlor held free of this trust.

  1. [Trustees' Power to Make Gifts at Discretion of

Settlors]{.underline}

A. Payments to Others From Community Property. The Settlors, acting

jointly, may at any time direct the Trustees in writing to pay

single sums or periodic payments out of the community property

in the trust estate to any other person or organization.

B. Payments to Others From Settlor's Separate Property. The

Settlor who has contributed separate property may direct the

Trustees in writing to pay single sums or periodic payments out

of that separate property to any other person or organization.

C. Power May be Exercised by Settlor's Conservator. Each

Settlor's power to so direct the Trustees shall be personal to

the Settlor, except that this power may be exercised by the

Settlor's conservator, or by the agent named in a ttlor's

ARTICLEV CAPACITY

  1. [Determination of Incapacity: Payment of Income and

Principal]{.underline}

If at any time, as certified in writing by two licensed physicians who

are not related by blood or marriage to either Settlor or any

beneficiary of this trust, either Settlor becomes physically or mentally

incapacitated, the Trustees shall pay to the other Settlor or apply for

the benefit of either Settlor as much of the net income and principal of

the trust estate as the Trustees consider necessary for the Settlor's

health, education, support, and maintenance. If at any time an owning

Settlor is not competent to make requests, the Trustees shall pay to or

apply for the benefit of the Settlors as much of the owning Settlor's

separate property trust property as the Trustees deem appropriate for

the Settlors' health, education, support, and maintenance in accordance

with their accustomed standard of living. In exercising their

discretion, the Trustees shall take into account that the Settlors

desire that property shall be applied for these purposes in a manner

similar to the applications the Settlors made while competent.

  1. [Payment by Conservator]{.underline}

The Trustees shall act in this manner whether or not a court of

competent jurisdiction has declared a Settlor incompetent or has

appointed a conservator. If a conservator has been

appointed fur a Settlor, however, the Trustees shall take into account

any payments made for that Settlor's benefit by the conservator.

  1. [HIPAA]{.underline}

When in the process of determining a Settlor's incapacity, all

individually identifiable health information and medical records may be

released to the person nominated as successor trustee, including any

written opinion relating to the Settlor's incapacity that the person so

nominated may have requested. This release authority applies to any

information governed by the Health Insurance Portability and

Accountability Act of 1996 (aka HIPAA), 42 USC 1320d and 45 CFR 160-164,

and applies even if that person has not yet been appointed successor

trustee.

  1. [Duration of Trustees' Power Relating to Incapacity]{.underline}

This Trustee power relating to incapacity shall remain in effect until,

as certified in writing by two licensed physicians who are not related

by blood or marriage to either Settlor or any beneficiary of this trust,

that incapacity is terminated and that Settlor is again able to manage

that Settlor's own affairs.

  1. [Long Term Care Facility Authorization]{.underline}

If either or both Settlors are in a long-term care facility (the ill or

disabled Settlor(s)), or if the Trustees believe in good faith that the

entry of one or both Settlors to such a facility is both necessary and

imminent, the Trustees may, in the Trustees' sole discretion, take any

or all of the following actions:

A. Transfer all or a portion of the Trust assets to the Settlor who is

not residing in or entering into a long-term care facility (the

community Settlor), or to that Settlor's attorney-in-fact, or if

both Settlors are residing in or entering long-term care facilities,

then to the attorney-in-fact of those Settlors, for the purpose of

making gifts. Such gifts are to be made when, in the discretion of

the community Settlor or the attorney-in-fact, (i) they would not

interfere with the entry to the long-term care facility which is

most appropriate and comfortable for the ill or disabled Settlor(s)

and (ii) they are necessary to preserve a portion of the ill or

disabled Settlor(s)' estate for beneficiaries named in this

revocable Trust. Such gifts, if made, shall be made to the same

persons, in the same portion, and on the same terms as the estate

would be distributed upon the death of the ill or disabled Settlor

under the terms of this Trust.

B. Make any changes and/or amendments to this revocable Trust to effect

the transfer of the ill or disabled Settlor(s)' interest in

Settlor(s)' personal residence to the

community Settlor. Such transfer is to take place in a manner and for

reasons that are consistent with the provisions of California Welfare

and Institutions Code Section 14006.2 or any replacement statute
