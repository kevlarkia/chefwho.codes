Workbook Structure:

Worksheet 2: Asset Register
A detailed register of all trust assets, including acquisition costs, market values, and ownership details.

Worksheet 3: Beneficiary Details
A section for listing all beneficiaries, their shares, and their relationship to the trust.


Formulas and Calculations:

Beneficiary Share: A formula to calculate the amount each beneficiary will receive based on their share percentage.
