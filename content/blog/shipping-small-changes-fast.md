date: "2026-04-28"
summary: "A repeatable way to ship in small, safe increments without losing momentum."

Large outcomes come from small, repeatable steps.

This site is being built with that same mindset:

1. Add foundational standards.
2. Ship a working scaffold.
3. Add one practical feature at a time.

Small changes keep review easy, reduce regressions, and let you learn from real
feedback quickly.
