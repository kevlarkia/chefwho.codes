## What it is built on

A rule, written down, runs before any model speaks. Then a record of
sources and entries. The journey stays inside each entry.

Three homes sit under that.

1. This browser. It is not enough.
2. A file of the record. It holds sources and entries. It does not hold
   the shelf, the talks, or the voice choice.
3. A storage space. Not switched on.

It is not built on a model's memory. It is not built on a public
database.

## The two weights

Source weighting is about one source.

- Its kind. Primary and record can carry. A witness can carry only at
  70 or above. Press, self, and hearsay inform only.
- Its standing. A number. It can be raised or lowered. Seasoned means
  50 or above.

Evidence weight is about one claim, after those sources are applied.

- An eligible source that supports it adds its full standing, and counts
  as a carrier.
- A source that supports it but cannot carry adds a quarter of its
  standing, and does not count as a carrier.
- A source that contests it subtracts its full standing.
- It is objective only when at least one eligible source carries it, it
  is not a fight, and the total meets the bar.
- The bar is the middle standing of the seasoned sources, held between
  58 and 80. Until three seasoned sources exist, the bar is 64.
- If you verify a claim the rule would not canonize, you hold it, and it
  stays subjective. That hold is not rewritten as proof. It is not
  dropped in silence if standing later moves.

## How a fight is measured

The notes say a contest that is close and strong enough is a fight, and
a fight is not objective. No separate cutoff for close or strong was
stored. The instrument uses the floor and the bar already set.

- Strong means the contesting weight is at least 58.
- Close means the gap between support and contest is at most the bar.

That measurement can be corrected when a cutoff is given. It is not a
new law.
